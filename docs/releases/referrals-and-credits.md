# Referrals & Credits

**Hosted feature release · September 25, 2026**

Share your referral link and get 5% back in credits on confirmed top-ups from friends who joined through it. Separately, send existing prepaid credits to another account by username.

## What ships and limitations

Referral rewards apply to credited deposits, not spending, and follow deposit corrections or reversals. Rewards are account credits, not cash or investment returns. Credit transfers require confirmation and cannot be undone; repeated request IDs cannot transfer the same credits twice.

## Film

The film uses original animated Greek ASCII mosaic laurels, a counting board and ledger stele. Accounts, deposit amounts, transfers and ledger entries are labeled examples.
